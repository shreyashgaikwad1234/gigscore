# Model card — GigScore

Generated by `src/train.py` on 2026-10-29; metrics from `reports/model_results.md` (same run). Evaluation, calibration, fairness and explainability sections are added in steps 4–5.

## Purpose and use

- **Predicts:** probability that a two-wheeler borrower misses the **first EMI** on its due date (L&T `loan_default`), at origination.
- **Intended use:** decision support for small-ticket two-wheeler loans to self-employed and new-to-credit applicants; a 300–900 score, approve/refer/decline band and reasons; shadow-mode pilots.
- **Not intended for:** fully automated declines without human review; loan types other than two-wheeler; pricing without recalibration on the lender's own book; any use of age or state as an input.

## Data

- L&T Vehicle Loan Default (Kaggle `mamtadhaker/lt-vehicle-loan-default-prediction`), real Indian NBFC loans.
- Training window: 01 Aug 2018 – 30 Sep 2018 (134,790 loans); tuning, tree counts and calibration by 5-fold CV inside this window.
- Out-of-time test: 02 Oct 2018 – 31 Oct 2018 (98,364 rows).
- Protected attributes (age, age band, state) are **not** model inputs; used only for fairness checks.
- `Employee_code_ID` not used. IDs (branch, supplier, manufacturer, pincode) are out-of-fold target encoded.

## Limitations

- Data is from 2018 and from one lender; default levels and ticket sizes are dated. Retrain on the deploying lender's book before use.
- Borrowers are self-employed / new-to-credit two-wheeler buyers ('gig-like'), not confirmed gig workers.
- The label is first-EMI default, which is noisy; expected out-of-time AUC on this data is 0.66–0.68.
- October's default rate (23.5%) is above the training window's (20.4%); probabilities calibrated on Aug–Sep under-predict October.
- No income or cash-flow data is available in L&T; the Watch layer and Account Aggregator data address that.

## Evaluation, explainability and fairness (step 4)

From `src/evaluate.py` on 2026-10-29; details in `reports/validation_report.md`.

- **Shipped model: lgbm_fair** (fallback scorecard_fair). It leaves out area, branch, dealer and ID-document features because they stand in for state and produced large state approval gaps in the all-features model; the cost is some accuracy (Section 2 and 8 of the validation report). lgbm_combined is the documented higher-accuracy alternative; switching is a config change (`model` in `config/thresholds.yaml`) and a lender and compliance decision. Chosen 2026-10-29 for the project's purpose: fair access for credit-invisible borrowers.
- **Headline:** for October applicants with no bureau score, lgbm_fair AUC exceeds bureau-only by +0.104 (95% CI +0.098 to +0.110).
- **Calibration:** isotonic on Aug–Sep out-of-fold predictions; as trained, October is under-predicted (observed 23.5%). Monthly recalibration backtest (offset fitted on 1–15 Oct, applied to 16–31 Oct): deciles outside ±2 pp 10 → 10 of 10. Offset is `calibration_offset` in `config/thresholds.yaml`.
- **Stability:** score PSI Oct 0.002, Nov 0.004.
- **Proposed bands** (lgbm_fair): approve ≥ 585, refer ≥ 569; October default rate approve 17.5%, refer 27.9%, decline 36.1%.
- **Explainability:** TreeSHAP reason codes; 100.0% of October applicants receive three distinct reasons. Most frequent decline reasons: The loan is a high share of the vehicle price (69%); The credit-bureau score is low (49%); The new EMI is large (46%).
- **Fairness:** 0 of 5 age band groups have an approval or FPR gap > 5 pp (max approval gap 3.4 pp).
- **Fairness:** 3 of 18 state groups have an approval or FPR gap > 5 pp (max approval gap 9.4 pp).
- **Fairness:** Shipped model vs all-features model at 60% approval: October AUC 0.658 → 0.637, largest state approval gap 34.0 → 8.5 pp, states flagged 9 → 3 of 18.
